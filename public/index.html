METHODOLOGY

Good decisions need more than a correlation chart.

We consider sample size, data completeness, confidence intervals, effect sizes, relevant controls, missing data, consistency, potential confounding, and limitations.

RAW DATA→QUALITY CHECKS→ANALYSIS→EVIDENCE→INSIGHT

EVIDENCE STRENGTH HIGH MEDIUM LOW Weak data should not produce overconfident conclusions.
